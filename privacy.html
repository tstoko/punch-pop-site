Punch Pop privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026. Applies to Punch Pop for iPhone.

In short: Punch Pop has no game accounts. The game shows ads from Google AdMob: an occasional ad between runs, and an optional ad you can choose to watch at game over for a second chance. Google may collect device and usage data to show ads and prevent fraud. A one-off Remove Ads purchase turns off the ads between runs. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you send.

Who we are

Punch Pop is made by Tom Stokolenko, publishing as stoko ("we"). You can contact us at hello@stokogames.com.

What the game stores on your device

Your best score, settings, the run you're playing and your Learn to Play progress are saved on your iPhone only. We can't see them, and deleting the app deletes them.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows two kinds of ad. Sometimes, when you start another run, an ad plays first; these never appear during a run, in your first session or in your first few runs. When a run ends, the game may also offer a WATCH AD button for a second chance, which plays an ad only if you tap it. To load and show ads, the game includes Google's Mobile Ads software, which may collect:

  • device and app identifiers, such as app- or developer-scoped identifiers
  • approximate location, worked out from your IP address
  • information about the ads you see and tap
  • app performance and crash information

Google uses this to show ads, measure them, keep ads safe and prevent fraud. Google is responsible for that data under its own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Remove Ads. A one-off purchase in the game turns off the ads between runs. The WATCH AD second chance stays available, and still plays only if you tap it.
  • Consent in the UK, EEA and Switzerland. When Google's consent system requires a choice, the game asks for it before requesting ads. Where available, you can revisit your choice in Settings → Privacy Choices.
  • Tracking. Punch Pop does not ask for Apple's permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Purchases

Remove Ads is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we don't receive your card or Apple ID details. The game uses Apple's purchase service to buy or restore Remove Ads and check whether you own it. It also keeps the ownership status on your iPhone.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use that information to respond to your request.

Apple

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may give us anonymous, combined statistics such as download counts and crash reports. See Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Punch Pop is a general-audience game and isn't directed at children. Ads are requested with Google's family-friendly content rating.

Your rights

You can email us with questions or requests about information you have sent us. For data collected by Google, use the links above or Google's My Ad Center.

Changes

If we change what the game collects, we'll update this page before that version is released and change the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@stokogames.com.
